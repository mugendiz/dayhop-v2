What's inside

  • Then and now. The history of the place you are about to stand in, from Wikipedia, credited.
  • Must-sees. The places you should not leave without seeing, whether or not you asked.
  • On during your trip. Events on your exact dates, with times, and the rest of that month.
  • Your days. A plan for each day: a morning outside, a midday stop, a place to eat, the evening's event.
  • Parks, trails and water. Conservation areas, lookouts and named hiking trails nearby.
  • By bike. Signed cycling routes and where to rent a bike.
  • By car, together. Scenic drives and day trips an hour or two out.
  • Sleeping under the sky. Campgrounds, RV and van sites, dump stations, highway service centres.
  • Eat, culture, play. The best-reviewed tables, museums, family stops and adventures.
  • Keepsake pages. A trip journal, a list of places you made it to, and room for ticket stubs.

Questions

Which places can I choose?

Any city, town or neighbourhood in Ontario on the list as you type: from Toronto neighbourhoods to Tobermory, Kingston to Thunder Bay.

How long can the trip be?

One day to five weeks, starting today or up to a year from now.

When does it arrive?

Usually within 15 minutes, by email, with a link that re-downloads it for a year. The thank-you page shows it the moment it is ready.

What if events are announced after I buy?

Write to admin@dayhop.quest the week before you leave and we will rebuild your guide with the latest listings, free.

Can I use it in my own city?

Yes. Pick a weekend at home and see it the way a visitor would. That is most of the fun.
