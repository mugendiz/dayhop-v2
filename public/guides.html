How does delivery work?

Checkout takes a minute (card, Apple Pay or Google Pay via Stripe). Your PDF downloads immediately on the confirmation page and lands in your inbox with a re-download link that also serves any refreshed edition.

Are these AI-generated lists?

No. Each guide is authored and hand-checked around stable, verifiable places, and every entry links to Google Maps as the live source for hours and closures. If something changed on the ground, tell us — admin@dayhop.quest — and the next edition fixes it.

What if a guide disappoints?
