Team 👥

Members sign in with their DayHop account (regular app sign-in, then open this page). Operators see the CRM plus reach metrics only — no revenue, no subscriber emails, no moderation. Deputies additionally see the accounting books and can receive ownership. There is exactly one owner — not removable, only transferable to a deputy. Every CRM action is logged with who did it.

Discovery inbox — new openings

Scans Canada-wide local media for newly-opened attractions, drafts catalog entries with Grok, then runs the autopilot verification chain: a Google Places closed-tag veto (only an explicit "closed" listing blocks) + the Grok editorial gate. Finds that pass publish to the live catalog automatically (✓); the rest wait here — verify facts yourself, then Copy catalog entry → data/curatedCatalog.js. The scan also runs on its own every Sunday at midnight (Toronto time).

Talk of the town — this week's outdoor picks

Every Sunday at midnight (Toronto) Grok searches the live web and X for the outdoor happenings people are flocking to this week, per region hub; each pick is verified (Google Places, ≤ ~2 h from the hub, dates, outdoors, no restaurant without a patio, nothing drinking-only) before it reaches the shelf. Toronto refreshes weekly; other hubs the first time someone browses from there. Pin a pick to carry it into next week; retire one to pull it now; add your own — it runs the same checks.
